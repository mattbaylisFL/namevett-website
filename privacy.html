Privacy Policy

Effective: May 2026 · App: NameVett (com.namevett.app)

Plain English

NameVett does not collect any personal data.

When you run a search, the name you typed is sent to several public services to perform the clearance check:

  • USPTO Open Data Portal (api.uspto.gov) — U.S. trademark search
  • EUIPO TMview (tmview.europa.eu) — EU trademark search
  • iTunes Search API (itunes.apple.com) — App Store and Apple Podcasts
  • RDAP domain registries — Verisign (.com/.net), Google Registry (.app/.dev), PIR (.org), Cloudflare DoH (.io/.co/.ai)
  • Public profile pages on Instagram, TikTok, GitHub, YouTube, and Reddit — to check whether a handle is taken

None of those services receive your name, your Apple ID, or any other identifier. They receive only the search term you typed. NameVett does not run its own server, so we never see, log, or store any of your queries.

Your search history is stored only on your device, in Apple's local SwiftData database. It is never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes all history.

The single in-app purchase (Unlock for $1.99) is processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit. Apple, not NameVett, handles all payment information. We never see your payment details.

On-device AI features

NameVett includes an AI summary, alternative-name suggestions, and a sounds-and-meaning audit. All three run on Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence). The on-device AI does not send any data to a server. Generation happens entirely on your iPhone.

Third-party services we communicate with

The services below receive only the search term you typed when you run a clearance check. Each has its own privacy policy that governs its handling of that request:

  • USPTO — uspto.gov/privacy-policy
  • EUIPO — euipo.europa.eu/data-protection
  • Apple iTunes Search — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Verisign / Google Registry / Cloudflare (RDAP & DoH) — operated as ICANN public protocols
  • Instagram, TikTok, GitHub, YouTube, Reddit — handle checks hit only the public profile URL

Apple's standard diagnostics

If you have Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share with App Developers turned on, Apple aggregates anonymous crash and performance metrics and forwards summaries to us through App Store Connect. We do not receive any user identifiers through that channel. You can turn it off at any time.

Children

NameVett is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to disclose, correct, or delete. You can delete your local search history at any time by removing the app or by deleting individual entries from the History tab.

Contact

Common questions are answered in the Support section of this site. NameVett does not run a server and does not collect data, so there is no contact endpoint beyond the public information published here.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will replace this page and the change will be noted in the app's What's New section in App Store Connect.
